About

The one person both sides trust.

Every project I've run had two sides that did not speak the same language: a state corporation and a university, an agency and an international federation, a Russian producer and an Asian regulator. My work is to be the one person both sides trust, and to keep the deal moving after the handshake.

I prepare the ground before a meeting, sit at the table during it, and take ownership of what was agreed afterwards: documents, approvals, partners, first deliveries. Companies work with me when they need someone on the other side of the border who is accountable for the result, not the process.

How I work

Five steps, and a finish line agreed up front.

  1. 01 · Weeks 1–2

    Diagnostic

    Who decides, who is worth meeting, what is realistic in this market. A written assessment you can act on.

  2. 02

    Partners and introductions

    A shortlist of partners, distributors or co-investors and warm introductions. First meetings in person.

  3. 03

    Negotiations and approvals

    I sit at the table on your side with ministries, regulators and associations through to agreed terms.

  4. 04

    Deal structure

    Trading company, incentives, quotas, contract and payment terms, so the deal works in both jurisdictions.

  5. 05 · Finish

    The agreed result

    Permits, logistics, customs, numbers. I stay until we reach the finish line we set.

Agreements are easy anywhere. Results take someone on the ground.
